## Full description (max 4000)
WealthSenseAI is a stock portfolio tracker with an AI analyst built in. Add the stocks you own, watch your net worth update with live quotes, and tap AI Audit for a buy, hold or sell signal on every holding, based on real fundamentals and this week's news.

TRACK YOUR PORTFOLIO
• Live quotes refresh every minute, so your net worth and every position stay current
• See each holding's price, daily change, shares and value at a glance
• Sort by ticker, price, daily change, value or shares
• No account or sign-up needed. Your portfolio stays on your device

AI SIGNALS ON EVERY STOCK
• AI Audit reviews each holding and gives a buy, hold or sell signal with a 12-month target price
• Every signal explains its reasoning, using live prices, P/E, earnings, 52-week range, revenue growth and dividend data
• Watch Mode re-runs the analysis automatically every few minutes while the app is open

NEWS ON YOUR STOCKS
• The most important recent headlines about the stocks you own, each summarized in one sentence
• Every story is rated bullish, bearish or neutral, with a link to the full article

IDEAS TO DIVERSIFY
• AI suggests stocks outside your portfolio that could balance or strengthen it, with the reason for each pick

ADD STOCKS IN SECONDS
• Smart Paste: copy positions from a brokerage statement and the AI pulls out tickers, shares and average cost
• Add by name: type "Tesla" or "Costco" and the AI finds the right ticker

PRIVACY
WealthSenseAI doesn't ask for your name, email or brokerage login, and your portfolio is stored on your device. Our privacy policy explains exactly what is sent when you use the AI features.

IMPORTANT
WealthSenseAI is for information and education only. AI-generated signals, target prices and suggestions can be wrong and are not investment advice. Franceschi Industries is not a registered investment adviser or broker-dealer. Always do your own research before making investment decisions.

WealthSenseAI is free and supported by ads.
